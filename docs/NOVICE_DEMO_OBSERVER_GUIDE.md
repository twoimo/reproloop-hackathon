# Private demo-only review: observer guide (NOT A RESULT)

Do not give this file to the participant until their unaided answers are saved.
A real observer must see a real new participant view the actual film. The
project author may observe but cannot act as the novice; record author presence
and any coaching/translation. An LLM may organize supplied records later but
cannot fabricate participation, answers, times, consent or sign-off.

## Distinct gates

- Demo-only comprehension: actual 180-second film + participant sheet. No SDK
  or device is needed. This does not test source installation or independence.
- Source setup: existing NOVICE_REVIEW.md with an appropriate host and separately
  authorized device steps. Do not require it just to start the demo-only check.
- Independent host: a different machine plus owner-granted exact access/target;
  no questionnaire or metadata report alone establishes that fact.

## Procedure

1. Verify the participant kit's manifest and film hash against the prepared
   receipt. The current recorded-v3 film is the preexisting 180-second demo;
   it does not depict the later fresh-AVD setup continuation.
2. Give only the film, participant sheet and neutral instructions. Keep this
   guide/source/status hidden until the answers are recorded.
3. Note start/end on one clock. Preserve pauses, rewatches, assistance and where
   the participant becomes confused. Do not help or correct during the review.
4. Ask all five questions in order. Save exact words, including wrong answers
   and "I don't know". Do not paraphrase away misunderstandings.
5. Interpret afterward against the evidence below. No automatic pass threshold
   or population/productivity claim is created by this guide or one participant.
6. Keep real observation separate from analysis. If no person participated,
   or observation/answers were not recorded, retain NOT_DONE.

- Q1: one self-owned F01 Cancel-persistence fixture family. Video-informed

  Android QA or unaided video/physical-input understanding.
- Q2: original v3 2/5 missed its >=3/5 goal; separate v4obs1 FINAL3/3 uses a
  different condition and correlated variants. Do not pool. Post-hoc B1 and B3
  3/3 with observed equal exports do not establish superiority or human speed.
- Q3: same export, fresh opposing builds, independent stored-state/event oracle
  within a trusted-local app/host boundary. Hashes and confirmation do not
  establish OS/producer/physical-touch attestation, other bugs or other machines.
- Q4: developer-supplied faulty/fixed logic, AI-assisted implementation and one
  build flag. ReproLoop did not automatically produce the repair.
- Q5: accept a genuinely evidence-supported limitation, not an expected slogan.
  Original README/status and demo source mapping are the factual references.

answer, quoting the words that justify the tag. Tags are not automatic study
